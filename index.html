[ 01 // THEORETICAL_GROUNDING ]

Algorithmic Philosophy

From first mathematical principles to deterministic computational execution.

Modern deep learning and machine learning often prioritize superficial heuristics over fundamental mathematical geometry. My work operates under a strict principle of first-principles derivation: rigorously analyzing the underlying topology of data manifolds, extreme value tail distributions, and continuous dynamical systems before compiling them into hardware-aware pipelines.

Whether synchronizing asynchronous order book ticks using backward join_asof without look-ahead leakage, engineering scratch Compact SVD protocols that preserve non-zero rank spectra, or deploying continuous-action Proximal Policy Optimization with custom Conjugate Gradient updates, the target is clear: maximum empirical edge with guaranteed mathematical stability.
